# Privacy Policy

Effective {{EFFECTIVE}} · Version {{VERSION}}

**In short: your messages, calls, contacts and screenshots are checked on your own device and never sent to us. We only receive a random app ID and, if you choose Deep check, the link you want checked. Second Look is free and paid for by ads from Google AdMob; ads never see your SMS, calls, documents or what you check.**

{{OPERATOR}} ("we") runs Second Look. This policy explains what personal data is involved, why, and your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

## 1. Data that stays on your device (we never receive it)

| Data | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Incoming SMS (sender and text) | Only while SMS Shield is ON (Android) | To check each new SMS for scams and warn you |
| Incoming caller's number | Only while Call Shield is ON | To show who's calling and warn about scam numbers |
| Text, links, numbers, UPI IDs you check | When you tap Check | To give you a result |
| Screenshots and QR photos you choose | When you pick one | Read on the device to find the payment details or QR code |
| Documents you choose to check (PDF or photo of an ID, passport, bank statement or letter) | Only when you pick one in Document check | Read on the device to look for signs of editing or wrong numbers. Never uploaded or stored; the temporary copy is deleted when you leave the screen. Aadhaar numbers are shown masked |
| Alerts list: every SMS and call the shields check, including ones that look normal (sender or number, time, result and, for SMS, a short preview with any OTP-style codes hidden) | While a shield is ON. You can switch "List every checked SMS and call" off to keep only suspicious ones | So you can see what was checked and act on it. Kept on the phone (up to 300 entries) until you delete them, one by one, by sender, or all at once |
| A list of senders seen before, stored only as one-way scrambled (salted hash) codes, not as numbers | While a shield is ON | To show a "New sender" badge. The numbers can't be read back from it |
| Numbers you marked (Block, Spam, Fraud, Safe) and your comments on them | When you mark a number | To block or label that number and show your history of marks. Not shared unless you separately report a number to warn others |
| Trusted person, settings | Saved by you | So the app can use them |
| How many checks you've started and when the last full-screen ad was shown (two numbers) | When you check something yourself | So ads follow the rules in section 4 (no ad after your first check, at most one full-screen ad every 2 minutes). Removed by "Delete all my data" |
| Crash reports (what went wrong inside the app, the app version, Android/iOS version and phone model; links and long numbers are removed) | Only when the app hits an error | So you can choose to send them to us with "Send report". Kept on the phone (newest 20), never sent automatically; "Clear" or "Delete all my data" removes them |

The optional call popup uses Android's "Display over other apps" permission, which you grant yourself, only to show who may be calling while an unknown number rings. The "Chat on WhatsApp" button only opens that app with the number; Second Look sends nothing to WhatsApp and can't know in advance whether the number uses it.

On a phone, alerts history, marked numbers and your trusted person are stored encrypted, and app data is excluded from cloud backups. On a laptop, this data stays in your browser's own storage on that computer. "Delete all my data" in Settings removes it. Second Look never reads your SMS inbox, call log or contacts. On the laptop web version inside Claude, a document photo you add there is read by AI on your own Claude account.

## 2. Data our server receives

| Data | When | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| A random install ID (not your name, number or device ID) | When you open Deep check or use community reports | To count your free deep checks and link your reports | Until you delete it in Settings, or 12 months without use |
| The link(s) you choose to deep check (never the message around them) | Only when you tap Deep check | To compare the link with Google Web Risk and phishing lists | Not stored; held in memory for up to 30 minutes to avoid repeat lookups |
| Daily deep-check count and ad credits | When you use Deep check | To apply the daily limit | 35 days |
| Ad reward confirmation from Google | Only if you watch an optional ad | To add your extra deep check, once | 90 days (to stop the same reward being used twice) |
| A number you report, the reason you pick, and your optional note and "who they said they were" | Only when you report a number and leave "Warn other Second Look users" on | Shown to other users so they can avoid the scam. Shown without your name; links, e-mail addresses and phone or account numbers are removed from notes | 12 months, or until you delete your data |
| A scrambled (hashed) form of your install ID next to each report | With each report | So each person counts once and you can delete your reports | Same as the report |
| A number you check | When you check a number with community reports on | To look up reports about it | Not stored |
| Your dispute ("this is my number") | Only if you add one | Shown next to reports about that number | 12 months |
| IP address and request time | Every server request | Security and abuse prevention | In memory for rate limits; hosting logs up to 180 days |

## 3. Who else processes data

- **Call Shield's community list** is downloaded to your phone. Incoming callers' numbers are matched on the phone and never sent to us.
- **Google Web Risk** receives the link you deep check.
- **Google AdMob** shows the ads in the app (see section 4) under Google's privacy policy.
- **Google ML Kit** (Android) reads screenshots on the device. The image is not sent to Google; ML Kit may send diagnostic data such as device model, app version and error codes.
- Our hosting provider stores the server data above, possibly outside India.

We don't sell personal data, use it for advertising profiles, or share it with anyone else except where the law requires.

## 4. Ads

Second Look is free. There are no purchases or subscriptions; ads pay for it.

- Ads are shown by **Google AdMob**, on phones only (the laptop version has no ads). You may see a banner at the bottom of Home, Check and Alerts, sponsored cards on Home, in Alerts and on the Offers tab, a full-screen ad after some checks you start yourself, and short video ads you choose to watch.
- AdMob may use your device's **advertising ID and device information** (such as model, operating system, app version and IP address) to show ads, limit how often you see them and measure them. We ask Google for non-personalised ads rated for general audiences.
- Users in regions that require it (for example the European Economic Area and the UK) are **asked for consent** before ads load, and can change it at any time in **Me › Privacy › Ad choices**. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
- **Ads never see your SMS, calls, documents or the content of your checks.** Nothing from the app's checks, alerts or marked numbers is put into an ad request.
- Ads never interrupt protection: none on SMS or call warnings, the call popup, "Being scammed?" help or onboarding, and no full-screen ad after a "Likely scam" result.

## 5. Consent and choices

- We process data based on your consent. SMS Shield, Call Shield, Deep check and sharing reports are each optional, and you choose the ad consent described in section 4 where it applies. Community reports can be switched off in Settings.
- You can withdraw consent at any time: switch a shield off, stop using Deep check, or delete your data. Withdrawing doesn't affect what was done before.

## 6. Your rights

- **Access:** Settings › Your data on our server shows everything the server keeps for your install.
- **Erasure:** Settings › Delete all my data deletes data on your device and on our server.
- **Correction, grievance and nomination:** email our Grievance Officer. We aim to reply within 24 hours and resolve within 15 days (and in any case within the time the law requires).
- If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## 7. Children

Second Look doesn't knowingly collect personal data from children beyond the random install ID needed for Deep check. Ads are never personalised and no behavioural tracking is done. If you are under 18, please use Second Look with a parent or guardian.

## 8. Security

Data on the device is encrypted with keys held in the phone's secure hardware. All connections use HTTPS. Ad rewards are verified on the server. If a personal data breach happens, we will inform affected users and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

## 9. Changes

We'll show you any important change in the app and ask you to accept it again.
