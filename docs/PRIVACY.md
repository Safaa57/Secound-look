# Privacy Policy

The Privacy Policy and Terms of Use shown inside the app are the official versions:

- `app/assets/legal/privacy.md`
- `app/assets/legal/terms.md`

Before publishing, also put both on a public web page (the Play Store and App Store require a privacy policy URL).
Fill in the operator details with build settings, as described in `docs/legal/LEGAL_CHECKLIST.md`.
